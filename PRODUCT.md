# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

The site markets a native iPhone app written in SwiftUI. The app itself is outside this repo.

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript served by GitHub Pages from `freycreates/ovnow-site`, with `CNAME` set to `ovnow.app`. No build step.

## Users

Primary: people in the Netherlands who ride buses, trams, metros, trains and ferries every day and want the next departure without friction. Often on foot, one-handed, in a hurry, outdoors. Dutch is the default language; English stays available by switch.

## Product Purpose

OVnow is a free iPhone app that shows every bus, tram, metro, train and ferry in the Netherlands live. It answers: how long until it leaves, where is it now, and what do I do on the way. Success on the website: Dutch riders understand it at once and want it when it launches.

## Positioning

A live departure, a live map and a Lock Screen journey in one app, with no account, no tracking and no ads. Free, with an optional tip.

## Operating Context

Countdown to the next departure with platform or pier and walking time; live vehicles on a map; Go starts a journey on the Lock Screen; journey planning with "get me home"; saved routes and Home Screen widgets; Lock Screen widgets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Status: coming soon to iPhone. Not downloadable yet. A hidden TestFlight button exists for later.
- The name is OVnow. The older spelling OVnu must not appear anywhere.
- iPhone only. Dutch and English.
- Live demo uses `/api/corridor` at `https://141-148-243-209.sslip.io`, with a clearly labelled timetable fallback.
- Analytics: GoatCounter, cookie-free, already on the site.
- Data credits are required: NDOV Loket, OVapi, OpenOV, MOTIS, Transitous (backup), OpenStreetMap contributors, Apple Maps.
- Contact: hello@ovnow.app.

## Brand Commitments

Name OVnow. Made by FREYcreates (freycreates.nl). Existing app icon: `img/icon.png`. Voice: short, direct, plain Dutch.

## Evidence on Hand

Real app screenshots in `img/shot-*.png`, which were captured before the rename and show the app in English. The real live Sprinter departure and positions between Amsterdam Centraal and Hoofddorp. No testimonials, press, ratings or download numbers exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Show what the rider needs now, and nothing else.
- Prove it live rather than describing it.
- Privacy is a plain fact, not a campaign.
- Be honest about status: coming soon means coming soon, and timetable data is labelled as such.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Colour never carries a status alone. Countdown stays legible outdoors. Respect reduced motion. Live regions must not spam screen readers.
