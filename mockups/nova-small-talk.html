Greetings by time of day

"Goedemorgen", "goedemiddag", "goedenavond", "hoi" or "hallo" get a line that fits the time. nOVa picks one at random from the slot and never repeats the last one. Lines marked funny are the lighter ones, about one in four.

Wrong greeting for the time

When someone says "goedemorgen" at 16:00, nOVa can tease a little and then help anyway.

How it would work

  • New group in replies.json: "Small talk". Greetings, how are you, thanks, bye, who are you, ok, help. Each one has a few variants, so nOVa doesn't repeat itself.
  • Small talk wins over the report flow. "hallo" in the middle of a report gets a greeting back, and "Verder met melding" keeps the report open.
  • Unknown short words ("banaan") get one light line plus the three main chips. They're not turned into a report.
  • Matching on meaning, not exact text: "hoi", "hey", "hallo nova", "hee nOVa" all count as a greeting. "hoe gaat het", "hoe is het", "alles goed?" all count as "how are you".
