Big stations: Departures | Station

Ideas 1, 2 and 3: tabs only at big stations, "big" decided by a rule, and opening on Train. Example data.

What counts as a big station

  • A train station with at least 2 other modes (metro, tram, bus or ferry), or more than about 30 departures an hour.
  • That catches Amsterdam Centraal, Zuid and Sloterdijk, Utrecht Centraal, Rotterdam Centraal, Den Haag Centraal and Schiphol, without a hand-made list.
  • A small station like Driebergen-Zeist, or a stop like Dam, keeps the current sheet.
  • Opening filter: Train the first time, then whatever you chose last at that station.
