How it behaves
  • Tap the bar: that stop opens, the map moves to it.
  • Walk 300 m on: the bar picks again from where you are (this already happens now).
  • Two favorites within 400 m: the closest one. Predictable beats clever.
  • The bar updates while you walk, but never while you are looking at a vehicle or a stop.
  • No location permission: the bar shows your first favorite, or "Search a stop".
