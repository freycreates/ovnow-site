Shops & food: where is it?

Ten ways to replace the long location line with something you can read at a glance. Same data in each: Amsterdam Zuid (Brittenpassage, platforms 1-2 and 3-4) or Amsterdam Centraal (West tunnel, Middle tunnel, IJ hall, East tunnel, track 2). Labels follow the phone language: English "Track 1 / West tunnel / IJ hall", Dutch "Spoor 1 / Westtunnel / IJhal".

A. Multi-location, collapsed chips

One Kiosk row lists its three spots as track chips, each with a status dot (green open, orange closing soon). Trade-off: status per spot is only a dot; tap to see times.

Recommendation

Go with D (badge on the right) as the default, plus F for shops with several spots (collapsed row, expands to one line per spot) and its quiet closed line: no extra line, reuses the yellow track chip, and the location reads like the departures list. Add H (on your platform) when a trip is active. For data, map each raw location text to a short key (track number, west/middle/east tunnel, IJ hall, passage, outside) and translate that key, so English and Dutch come for free. Skip C and E: too much per-station work or colour noise.

More ideas for D: the location badge

Option D keeps the location on the right, like the departures board. Ten ways to draw that badge without the track chip. Same Amsterdam Centraal and Zuid data in each.

Chosen: 4, calm version

A shop with several places shows no location until you open it (›); opened (⌄), each place gets its own pill and status, as in the light Zuid example. Shop names in plain white (dark mode) or black (light mode). The location pill is neutral grey with white or black text; only its small icon keeps a colour. Colour is left for what matters: the category icon and open or closed. Shown at three stations, in dark and light, Dutch and English.

Station icons for the location pill

The road-lanes icon looks like a highway. Six sets of station-style icons for the four kinds of spot (station hall, pedestrian tunnel, platform, passage), each shown in the calm grey pill with clean texts.
