Facility icons

One icon per facility type, all drawn the same way: a rounded tile in the type's colour with a dark line drawing on top. Colours are grouped, so you can tell at a glance whether it's food, a shop, getting around or a basic. Shop logos come later; these are the fallback when a shop has none.

How they're built

  • One shape: rounded square, same radius as the facility rows on the station sheet. Line drawings, 2 px stroke, round ends.
  • Colour by group: basics yellow (like NS signs), getting around blue/green, food warm orange/pink, shops lilac, services teal. Same group, same colour family.
  • Dark drawing on a light tile, so they read well in dark mode (where the station sheet lives) and in light mode.
  • No text in the icon except P (parking) and the "i", which are road-sign conventions people already know.

In the app

The same icons in the Station tab, in shops & food, and on light mode.
