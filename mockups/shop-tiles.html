Shop tiles, own-drawn

Each chain gets a tile in roughly its brand colour with its initials, in the same rounded shape as the facility icons. No logos are copied, so no permission is needed. A shop we don't know gets the plain facility icon (coffee, supermarket…). Three styles to choose from, then the same shops in the Shops & food list.

Things to keep in mind

  • Not logos: only colour plus initials or a simple mark, never a brand's drawing, mascot or font. That keeps it legally safe.
  • Fallback: unknown or local shops get the category icon from the facility set (lilac shop, orange coffee, pink food).
  • Data: which shops are at which station would come from the same source as the facilities (NS or OpenStreetMap). OpenStreetMap often has a "brand" tag, which is what the app would match on.
  • Easy to grow: adding a chain is one line: name, colour, initials, category.
