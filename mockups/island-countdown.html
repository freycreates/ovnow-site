Dynamic Island countdown

The current one: a white number with red "min" on top of a dark red ring, at about 14 pt inside a 37 pt circle.

Why it is hard to read now
  • Red "min" on a dark red ring on black has very little contrast, and at about 7 pt it is below what the eye reads at a glance.
  • The number sits on top of the ring, so the stroke cuts through the digits ("15" touches the ring on both sides).
  • Three things compete in one 37 pt spot: number, unit and progress. In a glance of half a second you only read one.
  • The red ring means "urgent" at 15 minutes too, so the colour says nothing.
  • In the wide island (screenshot 2) the right side is empty, while it could carry the text.

The last two minutes: seconds

"1:29" is four characters. That fits on the wide right side of the compact island, but not in the 37 pt circle. Ideas per state:

A. Compact: "1:29" with fixed-width digitsrecommended

15:31
1:29
  • Same spot and size as "8 min": only the text changes, and "min" falls away.
  • Fixed-width digits, so the text doesn't jump every second (the system timer text does this for you).
  • Same as Apple's Timer in the island; people already know it.

B. Compact: minutes until the last minute, then "45 s"

15:31
2 min45 s

Side by side: 1:29 shows as "2 min" (rounded up), under 60 s it shows "45 s"

  • Never a colon: one number and one unit, always.
  • Rounding up is safer: "1 min" at 1:29 makes people relax too early.
  • Downside: the jump from "2 min" straight to "59 s" feels sudden.

C. Minimal: number stays minutes, ring ticks secondsrecommended

15:31

Left: 1:29 ("2", ring for the minute). Right: 45 s ("45", ring for the last minute)

  • The circle only ever holds 1 or 2 digits, so it never gets crowded.
  • The ring shows the seconds moving, so you still see it counting down.
  • Under a minute the number becomes seconds; the red colour tells you that's the case.

D. Last 30 seconds: "Now"

Nu
  • Counting seconds at the door helps nobody: the doors close or they don't.
  • "Nu" (Now) fits both shapes and needs no reading.
  • Could be added on top of A or C.

With Music open

Two activities: iOS puts one inside the island and the other in a separate circle, and picks which. Options 6 and C in both spots, with the current one for comparison.

Now (for comparison)

15:25
  • The circle is the same size in both spots, so one design covers both.
  • Never more than 2 digits, no "min", and the ring sits at the edge, so it stays readable next to album art.
  • The colour (white, amber, red) is what you notice first when you glance at it.
Suggestion
  • Compact (island alone): option 1, with the colour rule from 3. In the last 2 minutes: A ("1:29").
  • Minimal (island shared): option 6. In the last 2 minutes: C (seconds on the ring, number at most 2 digits).
  • Both drop the red "min" on the ring, which is the main problem.
