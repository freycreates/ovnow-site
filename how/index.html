How OVnow works

OVnow is made by one person, with its own server. Here's how the app gets its live data, and what it takes to keep it running.

☕ Buy me a coffee To cover the costs, not to make money.

From timetable to your screen

Open transit dataTimetables, delays and vehicle positions, from NDOV Loket and OVapi.
→
The OVnow serverFetches it all every few seconds and gets it ready for the app.
→
Your iPhoneDepartures, live vehicles on the map and your ride on your Lock Screen.
  • The map and address search come from Apple Maps.
  • Trip planning runs through Transitous, an open journey planner.
  • The buildings and tracks in 3D come from OpenStreetMap and the 3D BAG. The big stations are built by hand.
  • You don't need an account. What happens with your location is in the privacy statement.

One server, run by me

OVnow runs on its own server in Amsterdam, not at a big company. It processes the live data for the whole of the Netherlands every few seconds, and easily handles a good crowd.

What it costs

  • ServerRuns day and night. I pay for it myself.
  • Domain nameovnow.app. I pay for it myself.
  • Apple DeveloperApple charges a yearly fee to put apps in the App Store. I pay for it myself.
  • TimeMany evenings and weekends to build the app and everything in 3D, and time every week to keep it up to date as timetables and data change.
