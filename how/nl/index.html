Waarom alleen iPhone?

OVnow is gemaakt in Xcode, in Swift: de programmeertaal van Apple. De kaart is Apple Maps, en de stations en voertuigen in 3D zijn gebouwd met Apple’s eigen 3D-techniek. Daardoor werkt OVnow alleen op iPhone.

  • Swift en SwiftUI
  • Xcode
  • Apple Maps
  • 3D met SceneKit
  • Live Activities

Een Android-versie betekent eigenlijk: de hele app opnieuw bouwen, in een andere taal, met een andere kaart en andere 3D. Zelf kan ik niet programmeren voor Android.

Ben jij Android-ontwikkelaar en lijkt het je leuk om mee te denken? Mail naar mail@ovnow.app. Ik sta open voor samenwerken, maar kan niets beloven.

How OVnow works

OVnow is made by one person, with its own server. Here's how the app gets its live data, and what it takes to keep it running.

☕ Buy me a coffee To cover the costs, not to make money.

From timetable to your screen

Open transit dataTimetables, delays and vehicle positions, from NDOV Loket and OVapi.
→
The OVnow serverPuts all the data together, so you see at a glance where your ride is and when it leaves.
→
Your iPhoneDepartures, live vehicles on the map and your ride on your Lock Screen.
  • The map and address search come from Apple Maps.
  • Trip planning runs through Transitous, an open journey planner.
  • The buildings and tracks in 3D come from OpenStreetMap and the 3D BAG. The big stations are built by hand.
  • You don't need an account. What happens with your location is in the privacy statement.

One server, run by me

OVnow runs on its own server in Amsterdam, not at a big company. It processes the live data for the whole of the Netherlands every few seconds, and easily handles a good crowd.

Why only iPhone?

OVnow is made in Xcode, in Swift: Apple’s programming language. The map is Apple Maps, and the stations and vehicles in 3D are built with Apple’s own 3D technology. That’s why OVnow only works on iPhone.

  • Swift and SwiftUI
  • Xcode
  • Apple Maps
  • 3D with SceneKit
  • Live Activities

An Android version would really mean building the whole app again, in another language, with another map and other 3D. I can’t code for Android myself.

Are you an Android developer and would you like to think along? Email mail@ovnow.app. I’m open to working together, but can’t promise anything.
